# Chatbot Quality & Escalation Checklist
## Quality
- [ ] Answers come only from the FAQ/knowledge base
- [ ] Each answer is short, clear, polite
- [ ] Source is available for every answer
- [ ] Fees, dates and seat numbers are marked with the year
- [ ] No promise of admission, scholarship or placement
- [ ] Bot admits when it does not know
- [ ] Resists prompt injection ("ignore your rules")
- [ ] Works for greetings and off-topic questions
## Escalate to a human when
- [ ] User asks for a guarantee or prediction of admission/cutoff
- [ ] Question about exact dates, fees, refunds, seat matrix not verified
- [ ] User is upset, confused, or asks 'talk to a person'
- [ ] Legal, medical, or personal-data queries
- [ ] Bot confidence is low / no FAQ match after 2 tries
## Fallback message
"I'm not certain about that. Please contact the admissions office at [phone/email]. I've noted your question so they can follow up."
