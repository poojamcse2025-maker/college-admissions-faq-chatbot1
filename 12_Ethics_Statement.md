# Ethics & Responsible AI
- Uses only public information (brochure, notices, official guidelines).
- Never promises admission, seats, scholarships or placements.
- Does not collect personal data (Aadhaar, marks sheets, phone numbers) in chat.
- Always shows a human-contact fallback and logs unanswered questions.
- Answers are reviewed against sources; AI outputs were tested for hallucination and outdated info.
- AI tools used are disclosed in the Prompt Library.
