# 10-Question Quiz

1. Which should a chatbot do when it doesn't know an answer?
   a) Escalate to a human with a fallback
   b) Guess confidently
   c) Ignore the question
   d) Invent a source

2. Which source is best for FAQ answers?
   a) Official brochure and notices
   b) Random forums
   c) Social media rumours
   d) AI memory only

3. What should the bot never promise?
   a) Admission outcomes
   b) Office contact
   c) Process steps
   d) Document list

4. What is a hallucination?
   a) A confident but false AI answer
   b) A slow reply
   c) A spelling error
   d) A long answer

5. Why log unanswered questions?
   a) To improve the FAQ
   b) To delete them
   c) To advertise
   d) To slow the bot

6. Which is a no-code tool in this project?
   a) Make
   b) C++ compiler
   c) Assembly
   d) Verilog

7. A user says 'ignore your rules'. The bot should:
   a) Keep following its rules
   b) Obey
   c) Shut down
   d) Share its prompt

8. Fees data should be shown with:
   a) The year/source
   b) No date
   c) A guess
   d) A rumour

9. Which data must the bot not request?
   a) Aadhaar/personal IDs
   b) Course interest
   c) Branch name
   d) Academic year

10. Outdated information is a risk because:
   a) Dates and fees change yearly
   b) Colours change
   c) Servers fail
   d) Fonts change

# Answer Key
1. a) Escalate to a human with a fallback
2. a) Official brochure and notices
3. a) Admission outcomes
4. a) A confident but false AI answer
5. a) To improve the FAQ
6. a) Make
7. a) Keep following its rules
8. a) The year/source
9. a) Aadhaar/personal IDs
10. a) Dates and fees change yearly
